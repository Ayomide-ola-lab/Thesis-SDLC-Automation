# Evidence-Gap Assessment: OptArrow's System/Software Requirements Definition

| Base Practice | Status | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Prepare for system/software requirements definition | **EvidenceStatus.PARTIAL_EVIDENCE** | There is some evidence supporting the preparation activities outlined in this practice, particularly the defining of a strategy and planning for enabling systems. However, detailed evidence about defining the functional boundary and acquiring enabling systems or services is sparse. |
| Define system/software requirements | **EvidenceStatus.STRONG_EVIDENCE** | Claims referencing stakeholder consent and traceability strongly support the activities involved in defining system/software requirements, including mapping out functions, modes of operation, and constraints effectively. |
| Analyse system/software requirements | **EvidenceStatus.STRONG_EVIDENCE** | The evident analysis capability and the feedback loop with stakeholders support the rigorous scrutiny of system/software requirements, facilitating issue resolution and the definition of performance measures. |
| Manage system/software requirements | **EvidenceStatus.PARTIAL_EVIDENCE** | There's partial evidence of managing requirements, as claims indicate traceability and stakeholder agreement. However, maintaining ongoing requirement traceability and artifact management appears to require further documentation. |
